A familiar problem

You know it is
somewhere in your files.

A circular saved last year. A paragraph in a long report. A table from a presentation. Office documents accumulate over years, across folders, formats and scanned pages.

Finding them can take longer than the work itself. Compass adds a local search layer over your existing files, so you can spend less time opening documents to check what is inside.

Keep your folders. Make their contents searchable.

Prepare once

Start with a folder you use.

Office documents

  • Employee leave policyPDF
  • Office handbookPDF
  • Staff application formsPDF

Example files, already in their own folder.

Add files or a folder to Compass, then select what to index. Compass prepares a local search copy; the original files stay where they are.

Large or scanned files take longer to prepare. You only repeat this when a file changes.

Works with
  • PDF
  • Word .docx
  • PowerPoint .pptx
  • Excel .xlsx
  • Text .txt
  • Markdown .md

Excel workbooks up to 5 MB. English OCR for scanned PDFs. PowerPoint images are not indexed.

Private by design

Your documents
don’t leave
your computer.

Indexing, OCR and search happen locally. Your documents are not uploaded to a server for processing.

  • Search your prepared library offline.
  • Keep control of the files you include.
  • Your original files remain unchanged.

Internet is needed to download Compass. Optional update checks contact GitHub, without sending your documents.

A tool for the work at hand

Less looking.
More getting on with it.

01Find what you partly remember.
Search by words or meaning, even when the filename has slipped your mind.
02Focus on the right files.
Search selected folders, formats or Favorites. Use Deep Search to look more closely within documents you select.
03Bring scanned pages into reach.
Built-in OCR makes readable scanned text searchable, alongside your other documents.
04Check the passage, then the source.
Follow a result to its source passage or page. Keep the original close when verifying a detail.
05Work with useful tables.
Extract and reconstruct tables from PDFs. Review the result against the source before copying it.
06Understand your growing library.
Review library health, exact and content duplicates, and search performance in local Analytics.

Start slowly

You don’t have to
index everything
on day one.

Indexing is a one-time preparation step for each file. It can take time, especially for large documents or scanned pages. Unchanged files are reused on later scans.

Add more when you are ready. Over time, Compass becomes your own searchable layer over years of work.

Keep source files in place where possible. Moving or renaming files or folders may require reconnecting them in your Library.

Before you install

System
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (x64). A compatible Microsoft Visual C++ x64 runtime is required.
Space
About 1 GB for Compass, plus room for your source files and local search index.
Windows notice
The current build is unsigned. Follow your organisation’s software and security policies.
Your library
Library data and settings are stored separately from the application. App updates do not require rebuilding an unchanged library.
