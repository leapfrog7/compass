A SIMPLE START

From scattered files
to answers you can check.

No new filing system. No uploads. Just your PDFs.

  1. 1

    Add a folder

    Choose a folder containing your PDFs. Originals stay untouched.

  2. 2

    Select and prepare

    Select the files you want and choose Index selected files. This first preparation can take time for large or scanned PDFs.

  3. 3

    Ask your question

    Search a topic in your own words. Compass finds relevant passages across the files you selected.

  4. 4

    Check the exact page

    Open the original source to verify it. Use Deep Search when you want to look more closely.

Prepare each PDF once. Compass reuses unchanged files on later scans. Keep your folders in place; moving or renaming them may require reconnecting files.

01 / FIND

Search across your PDFs.

Use keyword and meaning-based retrieval to find relevant passages in your selected files.

02 / VERIFY

Go straight to the source.

Open the original PDF and source page. Keep evidence close to every result.

03 / UNDERSTAND

Explore a little deeper.

Refine with Deep Search, reuse extracted tables, and read scanned pages with English OCR.

Ready for your
Windows workspace.

The installer includes the models needed for local search and OCR. Choose your PDF folders, select the files you want, and index them.

Internet is needed to download Compass and optionally check for updates. Office application policies still apply to this unsigned build.

Operating system
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (x64)
Storage
About 1 GB for Compass, plus room for your PDFs and local index
Runtime
Compatible Microsoft Visual C++ x64 runtime
Documents
PDF files; English OCR for scanned pages
Updates
Help → Check for updates. Close Compass, then install the newer version. Your saved knowledge stays in AppData.

Your documents stay yours.

Indexing, OCR and search run locally and work offline. Compass does not upload or change your source PDFs. Its database and settings stay separate from the application binaries. The optional update check contacts GitHub only when you request it and sends no document data.
